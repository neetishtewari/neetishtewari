<h1>Hi, I'm Neetish 👋</h1>

I'm a product manager who builds. I spend my days on AI-native B2B products, and I prototype them myself with AI coding tools so I can test an idea with working software before it reaches a roadmap.

I obsess over the user's problem first, then use design thinking and AI to solve it. That has taken me across B2B and B2C SaaS, mobile apps, and AI agents built to solve real business problems.

## What I'm building

Everything below is also on my [Product Lab](https://neetishtewari.co/product-lab).

**Live**

- **[Superfit](https://github.com/neetishtewari/superfit)** is a native Android fitness app for nutrition, training and physiology. You log meals and workouts by voice, a local intelligence layer works out readiness, and it runs on your own Gemini API key. [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.superfit.aifitness).
- **[Document Gem](https://github.com/neetishtewari/document-gem)** lets you upload, categorize, rate and chat with your documents. It detects invoices, contracts, receipts and notices, and pulls out the key fields. [Demo](https://documentgem.vercel.app/)
- **[Expense AI](https://github.com/neetishtewari/expense-ai)** checks expense receipts against company policy so teams can verify compliance faster. [Demo](https://expenseai.streamlit.app/)
- **[AeroSpeak](https://github.com/neetishtewari/aerospeak)** is an EdTech platform that uses AI voice agents. [Demo](https://aersospeak.vercel.app/)
- **[VizData](https://github.com/neetishtewari/vizdata)** is a data visualization platform. [Demo](https://vizdataboard.vercel.app/)

**In progress**

- **[FlickEd](https://github.com/neetishtewari/fickEd)** turns short-form video into structured learning paths for kids aged 8 to 12. Its early version is **[KidVid](https://github.com/neetishtewari/kidvid)**.
- **[DriveEx](https://github.com/neetishtewari/driveex)** is CRM technology for automotive.
- **[ResearchOS](https://github.com/neetishtewari/researchOS)** uses AI agents for market research.

I also keep a few machine learning notebooks on fraud detection: [InvoiceFraud](https://github.com/neetishtewari/InvoiceFraud) and [InsuranceFraud](https://github.com/neetishtewari/InsuranceFraud).
